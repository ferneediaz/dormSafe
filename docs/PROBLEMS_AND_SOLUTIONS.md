# DormSafe: 90-Second Pitch

## The problem (≈20 s)

Taiwan's dorms practise earthquake drills. When the alarm goes off, everyone evacuates and staff take a headcount. But in a real disaster, a missing name on that headcount doesn't tell staff whether the person is safe, trapped, injured or simply somewhere else. Reports of hazards arrive scattered across phone calls and LINE groups.

**In the first critical minutes, nobody has the full picture of who is safe, who is missing, who needs help, and which exits are blocked.**

## What goes wrong and how DormSafe fixes it (≈60 s)

| Problem | Solution | Where in the app |
|---|---|---|
| "Not checked in" could mean anything | One-tap check-in: **Safe / Inside / Help** | Resident → Emergency check-in |
| Staff don't know where to search | Live breakdown by floor and room | Coordinator → Floor matrix + **3D building** |
| Trapped or disabled residents get missed | **SOS** button and an automatically ranked rescue list | Resident → SOS; Coordinator → Priority rescue list |
| A roommate knows someone is safe, but it isn't recorded | Peer confirmation | Resident → "My roommate is safe with me" |
| Hazard reports scattered across chats | Photo reports with a status workflow | Resident → Report a problem; Coordinator → Reports |
| People head for blocked stairs | Route status, with staff verification | Coordinator → Stairwells & routes |
| Responders arrive to a verbal briefing | **One-click 119 handoff** (copy / print) | Coordinator → Responder handoff |
| Networks go down | Offline queue that syncs automatically | Header → Online/Offline |
| Migrant workers can't read Chinese | 繁中 / EN / Filipino / Indonesia | Header → language switcher |
| Personal data exposed (PDPA) | Role-based access, auto-deletion | Coordinator → Access role, Privacy tab |

Built on **real Taiwan open data**: NCDR disaster alerts in the header banner, and NFA shelters with walking directions on the Resident page.

## Close (≈10 s)

> **DormSafe turns a headcount into a live picture of who's safe, who needs help, and where responders should go first.**
